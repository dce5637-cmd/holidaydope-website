Day Wise Itinerary

Day 1 — Arrival in Dubai

Arrive at Dubai International Airport and meet our representative. Enjoy a smooth transfer to your hotel, complete check-in and spend the evening at leisure exploring nearby cafes, malls or Dubai nightlife.

Day 2 — Dubai City Tour & Burj Khalifa

After breakfast, enjoy a Dubai city tour covering famous landmarks and photo stops. Later, visit Dubai Mall and experience the iconic Burj Khalifa with stunning city views.

Day 3 — Desert Safari & BBQ Dinner

Enjoy a thrilling desert safari experience with dune bashing, sunset photography, cultural performances and BBQ dinner at a desert camp for a memorable Arabian evening.

Day 4 — Dhow Cruise & Shopping Experience

Spend your day shopping at Dubai’s popular malls and markets. In the evening, enjoy a beautiful dhow cruise dinner with skyline views, music and a relaxed luxury vibe.

Day 5 — Departure

Enjoy breakfast, complete checkout formalities and transfer to the airport with unforgettable Dubai memories.
