Day Wise Itinerary

Day 1 — Arrival in Srinagar

Arrive at Srinagar Airport and meet our representative. Transfer to your hotel or houseboat, complete check-in and enjoy a peaceful evening near Dal Lake.

Day 2 — Gulmarg Snow & Gondola Experience

After breakfast, proceed for a full-day Gulmarg tour. Enjoy snow views, mountain landscapes and optional Gondola ride experience. Return to Srinagar by evening.

Day 3 — Sonmarg Valley Tour

Explore Sonmarg, known for golden meadows, river views and beautiful valley landscapes. Enjoy photography, local sightseeing and peaceful mountain vibes.

Day 4 — Dal Lake Shikara Ride & Shopping

Enjoy a relaxing Shikara ride on Dal Lake, visit local markets for Kashmiri handicrafts, dry fruits and souvenirs, then spend the evening at leisure.

Day 5 — Departure

Enjoy breakfast, complete checkout and transfer to Srinagar Airport with unforgettable Kashmir memories.
