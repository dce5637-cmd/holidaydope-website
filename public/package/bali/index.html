Day Wise Itinerary

Day 1 — Arrival in Bali

Arrive at Bali International Airport and meet our representative. Enjoy a comfortable transfer to your hotel or private villa. Complete check-in, relax after your journey and spend the evening at leisure with Bali’s tropical atmosphere.

Day 2 — Ubud Tour, Waterfall & Rice Terrace

After breakfast, explore the cultural side of Bali. Visit Ubud, enjoy beautiful rice terrace views, explore a scenic waterfall and experience Bali’s peaceful nature, local art and traditional island charm.

Day 3 — Nusa Penida Island Tour

Start early for a full-day Nusa Penida island tour. Visit famous scenic spots, enjoy breathtaking cliffs, crystal blue water and photo-worthy viewpoints. Return to Bali in the evening for overnight stay.

Day 4 — Beach Club, Temple Visit & Sunset

Enjoy a relaxed day with beach club vibes, coastal views and a beautiful temple visit. End your day with a magical Bali sunset and optional romantic dinner or nightlife experience.

Day 5 — Shopping, Leisure & Departure

After breakfast, enjoy free time for shopping, cafes or leisure activities depending on your flight timing. Later, transfer to the airport with beautiful Bali memories.
