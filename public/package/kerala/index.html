Day Wise Itinerary

Day 1 — Arrival & Munnar Hotel Check-In

Arrive at the airport and meet our representative. Drive towards Munnar through scenic hills, waterfalls and tea gardens. Check in to your hotel and relax in the peaceful mountain atmosphere.

Day 2 — Tea Garden & Waterfall Sightseeing

After breakfast, explore Munnar attractions including tea gardens, viewpoints, waterfalls and local sightseeing spots. Enjoy beautiful nature views and photography.

Day 3 — Alleppey Houseboat Experience

Proceed to Alleppey and check in to your houseboat or backwater stay. Enjoy Kerala’s peaceful backwaters, traditional meals and relaxing water views.

Day 4 — Backwater Relaxation & Local Tour

Enjoy breakfast and spend the day relaxing around the backwaters. Explore nearby local attractions, markets and peaceful village vibes.

Day 5 — Departure

Enjoy breakfast, complete checkout and transfer to the airport/railway station with unforgettable Kerala memories.
