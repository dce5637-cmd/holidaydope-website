Thailand Trip Cost From India

Budget planning, hotel prices, island tours and Thailand travel tips for Indian travellers.

Thailand is one of the most affordable international travel destinations for Indian travellers. Beautiful beaches, nightlife, island tours, shopping and luxury resorts make Thailand a perfect destination for couples, families and friends.

If you are planning a Thailand vacation, understanding the total trip cost can help you choose the right package and travel style.

Thailand Package Cost From India

Thailand packages from India generally start from around ₹19,999 depending on destination, hotel category and activities included.

  • Bangkok + Pattaya: Starting from ₹19,999
  • Phuket + Krabi: Starting from ₹29,999
  • Luxury Thailand trip: ₹45,000+

Thailand Flight Cost

Round-trip flights from India to Thailand usually cost between ₹15,000 to ₹30,000 depending on season and departure city.

Booking flights early can help reduce the overall travel cost.

Quick Recommendation

For budget travellers, Bangkok and Pattaya are more affordable. For island luxury experience, Phuket and Krabi are the best options.

Hotel Cost In Thailand

Thailand offers hotels for every budget category.

  • Budget hotels: ₹2,000 – ₹4,000 per night
  • Mid-range hotels: ₹5,000 – ₹10,000 per night
  • Luxury resorts: ₹15,000+ per night

Best Places To Visit In Thailand

  • Bangkok – shopping and nightlife
  • Pattaya – beaches and water activities
  • Phuket – luxury island destination
  • Krabi – scenic beaches and island tours
  • Phi Phi Islands – famous island experience

Best Time To Visit Thailand

The best time to visit Thailand is from November to March. During this season, the weather is pleasant and ideal for sightseeing and island tours.

This is also the most popular travel season, so early booking is recommended.

How Many Days Are Enough For Thailand?

A 4 nights and 5 days Thailand trip is perfect for most travellers.

  • 4N/5D – Best for Bangkok + Pattaya
  • 5N/6D – Best for Phuket + Krabi
  • 6N/7D – Relaxed luxury Thailand vacation

Thailand Travel Tips

  • Carry some Thai Baht for local shopping.
  • Book island tours in advance during peak season.
  • Carry beachwear and sunscreen.
  • Keep passport and travel documents safe.
  • Choose hotel location carefully for easier sightseeing.
